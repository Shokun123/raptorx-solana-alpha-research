## 1. What It Is

**Drift** (ticker: **$DRIFT**) is a decentralized perpetual exchange (perps DEX) built on the Solana blockchain. The protocol enables users to trade perpetual futures with leverage, provide liquidity, and earn yield, all within a non-custodial environment. Drift has been a notable player in the Solana DeFi ecosystem, known for its innovative risk management and cross-margining features.

The project experienced a significant security exploit earlier in its lifecycle, resulting in a temporary loss of user funds. In response, the Drift team implemented a comprehensive recovery plan, including a **User Safety Fund (USDT)** to pledge paybacks and a full security redesign. The protocol is now in a **relaunch phase**, aiming to return as a more secure and feature-rich trading platform.
