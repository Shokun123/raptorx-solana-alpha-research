# Technical Analysis Report: Orca (ORCA)

## 1. What It Is

**Orca** (ticker: **ORCA**) is a decentralized exchange (DEX) protocol built natively on the Solana blockchain. The project operates under the contract address `orcaEKTdK7LKz57vaAYr9QeNsVEPfiu6QeMU1kektZE` and is one of the earliest and most established DeFi protocols on Solana, having launched its trading pair on November 28, 2022.

Orca is designed as a user-friendly, efficient automated market maker (AMM) that leverages Solana's high-speed, low-cost infrastructure to provide seamless token swaps. The platform is known for its "concentrated liquidity" model, which allows liquidity providers to allocate capital within specific price ranges for enhanced capital efficiency. The project maintains an active presence through its official website (orca.so), comprehensive documentation (docs.orca.so), and community channels including X (formerly Twitter) at @orca_so and a Discord server.

The token is currently trading against Wrapped SOL (WSOL) on the Orca DEX itself, with the pair address `2ZnVuidTHpi5WWKUwFXauYGhvdT9jRKYv5MDahtbwtYr`. With a fully diluted valuation of approximately $128.2 million and a market capitalization near $78 million, Orca represents a mid-cap DeFi protocol with meaningful liquidity depth on its native exchange.
